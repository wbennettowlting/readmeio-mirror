Harbor supports three transfer types. Use the table below to find the one you need, then follow the link for full coverage details.

| Transfer type | What it does | Available in | Payment methods |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [**On-Ramp**](/docs/on-ramp-coverage) | Fiat → stablecoin | US only | ACH Pull, Wire, Debit Card |
| [**Off-Ramp**](/docs/off-ramp-coverage) | Stablecoin → fiat | 87 countries across the Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and Middle East & Africa | Local bank rails and wires |
| [**On-Chain**](/docs/on-chain-coverage) | Stablecoin → stablecoin | All supported blockchains | Blockchain to blockchain |

### Stablecoins and blockchains

* **USDC** is supported on all 9 blockchains: Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Avalanche, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Arc, and Stellar.
* **USDT** is supported on **Ethereum** only.

See [**Stablecoins and Blockchains**](/docs/stablecoins-and-blockchains) for blockchain keys, confirmation times, and testnets.

### Limits

The pages in this section give a general overview. Exact limits and supported currency pairs depend on your application's configuration. See [**Limits and Live Coverage**](/docs/limits-and-live-coverage) to retrieve them through the API.
