An on-ramp converts fiat currency into a stablecoin. On-ramp is currently available to **US Customers only**.



| Country | Currency | Payment methods |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| United States (US) | USD | ACH Pull, Wire, Debit Card |


<br/>
Funds can be delivered as USDC on any [supported blockchain](/docs/stablecoins-and-blockchains), or as USDT on Ethereum.

To fund on-ramp transfers by ACH Pull or debit card, the Customer first links a bank account or card. See [**Linked Funding Sources**](/docs/linked-funding-sources).

For eligible card networks and card types, see [**Debit Card Coverage**](/docs/debit-card-coverage).
