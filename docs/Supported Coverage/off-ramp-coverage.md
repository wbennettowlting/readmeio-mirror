An off-ramp converts a stablecoin into fiat currency and pays it out to a bank account or card. Find the destination country below to see which currencies and payment methods are available.

Unless noted otherwise, every route supports:

* **Stablecoins:** USDC on all [supported blockchains](/docs/stablecoins-and-blockchains), and USDT on Ethereum.
* **Sender and recipient types:** all combinations of individuals and businesses (B2B, B2C, C2B, and C2C). See [Restrictions](#restrictions) for exceptions.

### Restrictions

Some payment methods are only available for certain sender and recipient types:

| Country | Currency | Payment method | Supported types |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hong Kong (HK) | HKD | FPS | B2B only |
| Hong Kong (HK) | USD | CHATS | B2B only |
| India (IN) | INR | IMPS, NEFT, RTGS | B2C and C2C only |
| Vietnam (VN) | VND | Local Bank Transfer | B2B, B2C, and C2C (no C2B) |

<br />

For local currency payouts such as HKD, SGD, MXN, and BRL, see [**Local Currency Transfers**](/docs/local-currency-transfers). For debit card payouts, see [**Debit Card Coverage**](/docs/debit-card-coverage).

Limits and supported pairs can vary by application. See [**Limits and Live Coverage**](/docs/limits-and-live-coverage) to retrieve your exact settings.
